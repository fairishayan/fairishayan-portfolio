# The Psychology of Love [Redefined]

> **TL;DR:** Love isn't a single feeling. It's a compound of Trust, Care, and Support. And when we break it down honestly, we find two very different kinds — Planned and Natural — with completely different psychological consequences.

## What is Love?

Mere nazariye se, pyaar humare emotions ka ek part hai — jo mil kar bana hai **teen cheezon** se:

1. **Trust**
2. **Care**
3. **Support** (Mental, Physical, Financial)

Hum kisi se pyaar karte hain, ya koi humse pyaar karta hai — ye hume realize tab hota hai jab humare beech ye teen cheezein ho.

## Pyaar Sirf Couples Me Nahi Hota

Pyaar sirf romantic relationships tak mehdood nahi hai. Wo har rishtey me hota hai:

- Siblings
- Cousins
- Parents
- Relatives
- Friends
- Close ones
- Mentors

Aur agar baat karein **couples** ki — toh isme maine **do categories** create ki hain.

## Do Categories of Love

### 1. Planned Love

Mostly jab hum kisi ko jante hain, unse humari vibe match hoti hai, humare beech naturally strong bonding hoti hai — **phir hum decide karte hain** ki hume is insan se pyaar karna chahiye aur apna life partner banana chahiye.

**Characteristics:**

- Hum ya humara partner **decide** karta hai ki pyaar karna hai ya nahi
- Kab, kaise, kya karna hai — sab **controlled**
- Hum apne emotions aur feelings ko **control** kar lete hain
- Hum khud ko kuch aur **pretend** kar sakte hain — jo hum na kabhi the, na kabhi ban sakenge
- Agar saath cheat ho jaye toh **easily move on** kar sakte hain

### 2. Natural Love

Ye basically "love at first sight" ki tarah nahi hai — ye kuch aur hai. Jaise mujhe **usse** hua.

Jab maine use pehli baar dekha, jo maine feel kiya wo usse pehle kabhi nahi feel hua. Mujhe uske baare me kuch nahi pata tha. Fir bhi main uske peeche gaya.

**Characteristics:**

- Humare emotions humare **control me puri tarah se nahi** hote
- Kabhi gusse me baat na karna, fir baad me sorry bolna — ye natural love me hota hai (though ye prove nahi kiya ja sakta, kyunki pretend bhi possible hai)
- Hum khud ko **chah kr bhi pretend nahi kar sakte**
- Aksar hum duniya walon ke liye kuch aur, apne close ones ke liye kuch aur hote hain — just because we can't pretend our personality
- Agar saath cheat ho jaye — **severe anxiety, depression** se koi nahi bacha sakta

## What's Different in My Love?

Mujhe bhi usse pyaar hai. Mai bhi usko follow karta tha class me. Bas usko hi chupke chupke dekhta rehta tha 🤭. Maine kabhi uska face nahi dekha, fir bhi mujhe usse pyaar hai.

Aur humans ka nature hai — jab hume kisi se sachha pyaar hota hai, toh hume us insan ki **har ek quality** dikhti hai. Uski qualities:

- **Calm nature** — pehle observe karti hai, fir react karti hai
- **Selective access** — baaki ladkiyon ki tarah sabko apna access nahi deti; sirf close ones ko emotions ka access deti hai
- **Time-conscious** — faltu seminars/events me time waste nahi karti; bas sahi guidance chahiye, fir apni saari energy aur efforts ek hi jagah consistency se laga sakti hai
- **Independent** — kabhi dusron ke sahare pe depend nahi rehti
- **Introvert** — kisi ka sahara nahi dhoondhti. Apni problems khud handle karti hai
- **Logical over emotional** — ek drawback hai jo har insan me hota hai: jab baat **religion** ki hoti hai, wo logical se zyada emotional ho jati hai
- **Curious** — life, people, their thinking, and desires ke baare me

Aur wo ek **pure heart and calm nature** wali ladki hai. Mujhe ye sab usko observe karke pata chala — aur fir mujhe usse pyaar hua.

**Lekin** main kabhi usse return me pyaar ya kuch bhi expect nahi karta tha. Ab main believe kar chuka hoon — **she's the rarest human of this generation.**

Aur main bas ek cheez expect karta hoon: **kaash wo bhi mujhe choose karle**.

I know expect karna galat hai — lekin uske baare me soch kar mujhme **himmat, motivation, aur discipline** aa jati hai.

## Expectations — Honestly

Main bas ye expect karta hoon ki wo mujhe **as a life partner, as a husband** choose kare. Lekin:

- **Forcefully nahi** — balki apni marzi se choose kare
- Mujhe usse pyaar hai — iska matlab ye nahi ki wo mere pyaar ki care kare, ya respect kare, ya kuch bhi kare
- Mere perspective se **wo bhi ek insan hai**, aur usko apni life ka har decision **khud se lene ka haqq** hai
- Jo usse main ya koi bhi **chheen nahi sakta**
- Main bas ek **hope** kar sakta hoon, apne intentions pure rakh sakta hoon, apni thinking ko biased hone se rok sakta hoon

## Ek Line Me

Agar ek line me bolun — **mere pyaar me kya farq hai** — toh:

> Maine kabhi duaon me us insan ko **nahi** manga.
>
> Par hamesha uske liye bohot saari **khushiyan**, achhi zindagi, aur usko apni life ka har ek decision lene ki **freedom** maangi hai.
>
> Aur uske dukhon ka **ek hissa apne liye** zaroor manga hai.

## Closing

So in the end — mujhe pyaar hai, aur mujhe **sahi aur galat ke beech ka farq** bhi pata hai.

Wo khush hai, bas itna kaafi hai mere liye. Main chahe kaise bhi phase se guzar jaun, kitna bhi depressed ho jaun — meri koshish bas yehi rahegi:

- Usko **kabhi hurt na karun**
- Jab usko **meri zarurat ho**, main available rahun

Kyunki mujhe har kisi se nahi hota pyaar. Toh jisse mujhe pyaar hai, mai at least apni life me itne efforts kar sakta hoon uske liye.

Mujhe use preshan karne me maza aata hai — but wo mere saath utna bhi friendly nahi hai ki mai kuch bhi mazaak kar sakun. But main karta hoon — irritate usko kabhi "mem saab" bol kr, kabhi "ullu", kabhi "babu", kabhi "gulabo". 🤭

So from my aspect — **this is what love looks like**.
